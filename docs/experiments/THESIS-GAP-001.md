## Scientific assessment

The primary character-SVC + CRF had 1,206/2,033 exact frames on dev and 1,733/2,974 on test. Roughly two in five test frames were incomplete: 777 had correct intent only, 295 correct slots only and 169 neither. Intent accuracy was stable (84.4073% dev, 84.3981% test), while intent macro-F1 fell from 82.2898% to 80.2292% and slot micro-F1 from 72.2467% to 71.2539%. The CRF missed 981 gold spans and added 477 spurious ones. One predicted BIO transition required repair; all test gold spans were representable. Both full CRF runs reached their iteration caps. These outcomes support a measured baseline without establishing deployment suitability, semantic correctness of the source, or optimizer convergence. [Published aggregate](../../results/EVAL-FREEZE-001/held-out-breakdowns.md) · [Controlled CRF comparison](SLOT-CRF-002.md)

The hybrid had five additional exact frames on dev and twelve fewer on test. The prespecified primary remained fixed. The paired hybrid-minus-primary difference was −0.4035 percentage points (95% interval −1.0424 to +0.2354). Primary test joint accuracy was 205/230 on exact text seen in train and 1,528/2,744 on unseen text. The overlap subgroups differ in composition. Exact duplicates alone do not establish improper leakage. `audio_volume_other` has no dev intent support and `cooking_query` has no test intent support. `game_type` and `sport_type` have zero test slot support. Distinguish no observations from observed failures such as one gold `music_album` span missed. The 2,000 seed-42 utterance bootstrap draws condition on the fitted models. They do not capture related templates, training-seed variation, label uncertainty or transfer to another domain. These are descriptive subgroup findings. [Frozen protocol](EVAL-FREEZE-001.md)

## Multilingual encoder feasibility decision

A joint XLM-R-base baseline could test whether contextual multilingual representations improve intent and slot decisions under the same Azerbaijani supervision. Such a comparison would change the representation, model family and training objective together; it could not isolate the effect of multilingual pretraining. XLM-R-base has 12 layers of width 768 and a 250,002-entry vocabulary. A planning estimate of about 270–280 million parameters implies roughly 4.3–4.5 GB decimal for FP32 weights, gradients and two Adam moment buffers alone, before activations, framework allocations and the OS. This is a memory estimate, not a benchmark. [Published architecture](https://huggingface.co/FacebookAI/xlm-roberta-base/blob/main/config.json) · [XLM-R paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.747/)

The local host reported Apple M1, 8 CPU/8 GPU cores, 8 GB unified memory and roughly 29 GiB available disk. CPython 3.12.4 has torch 2.5.1, transformers 4.46.2, tokenizers 0.20.3 and huggingface-hub 0.26.2. The CRF package lives in a separate local environment. Torch reported MPS built, runtime unavailable in this session. SentencePiece and datasets were absent; a fast tokenizer with a verified tokenizer JSON and the existing JSONL reader could avoid those dependencies. No checkpoint was loaded, downloaded or timed. Full fine-tuning on this host is unverified and memory constrained. CPU time and feasible batch size remain unknown.

Prioritize semantic review and packaging in Thesis I. For Thesis II, confirm supervisor scope and compute, pin checkpoint revision/license and environment, and declare a bounded train/dev-only plan. Implement a shared encoder with intent and token heads and a declared subword-to-source-offset policy; test truncation, padding and exact span reconstruction before any training. Predeclare seed(s), epochs, batch budget and stopping rule. A future authorized short train-only pilot should measure peak memory and seconds per step. For scale, three epochs at effective batch 8 on 11,514 examples require about 4,320 optimizer steps per seed, or 12,960 for three seeds. These are planning counts; gradient accumulation can require multiple forward/backward passes per step. Use the existing dev IDs, strict typed spans and frame metrics for exploratory comparisons. Any new confirmatory evaluation needs genuinely untouched data. Preserve the published official test benchmark and primary/comparison assignments.
